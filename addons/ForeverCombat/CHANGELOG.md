# ForeverCombat

## Unreleased

- Initial version.
- **DoT meter:** your damage over time spells mark the damage they still have to deal on your
  target's and focus's health bar, one segment per spell. When they cover all of the remaining
  health, the bar's end glows: they'll finish it off.
- **Red portrait on kill:** while your damage over time spells will finish off your target or
  focus, its portrait turns red, so you know at a glance to switch targets. Turn it off under
  DoT Meter in the options.
- **HoT meter:** your heal over time spells extend a friendly target's or focus's health bar by
  the healing they still have to do. When they reach the end of the bar, it glows: they'll heal
  it to full.
- Hover the target or focus frame to see each spell's amount and time left.
- Tracks the base class spells and talents of every class. Each segment shrinks as your ticks
  land. The game keeps tick sizes hidden, so amounts are the spell tooltip's, without your spell
  or attack power.
