# ForeverTooltip

## Unreleased

- When you hover a player, their name shows in their class colour.
- The level line below it shows whether they're friend or foe: red for enemies, yellow for players you can attack who aren't hostile, green for allies.
- New option in Options → AddOns → ForeverTooltip: "Tooltip follows the cursor" shows tooltips for players, creatures and objects in the world next to your mouse instead of at the usual tooltip spot. Off by default.
