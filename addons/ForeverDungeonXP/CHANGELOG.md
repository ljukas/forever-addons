# ForeverDungeonXP

## Unreleased

- A journal of your dungeon runs: the experience each run's kills gave you, how big a share of a
  level that was, the kills per creature, the time from first to last kill and the experience
  per hour. Open it from the minimap button, the addon menu on the minimap or with `/fdxp`.
- Elites, rares and bosses are marked in a run's creature list with the dragon and skull icons,
  for runs recorded from this version on. Click a column heading to sort the list by it; click
  again to turn the order.
- A run shows who you ran it with: each party member's name and level in their class colour,
  with class and group role in the line's tooltip. Recorded for runs from this version on.
- The journal shows the runs of all your characters: pick one from the dropdown above the run
  list, without logging over to it, or "All characters" to see everyone's runs together.
- A run shows how much of its kill experience was rested bonus, and its experience per hour
  with and without it. Recorded for runs from this version on.
- A run has a timeline: its first and last kill, each boss defeated with how long the fight took,
  wipes, deaths, level-ups and who got each rare or better item someone rolled Need on, by the
  time into the run.
  Switch between Creatures and Timeline with the tabs under the journal. Recorded for runs from
  this version on.
- The run list is grouped by dungeon or by the level you were when the run started: click the
  Dungeon or Level heading above it, and click again to turn the order.
- The run list has three columns, Dungeon, Level and XP/h, each under its own heading. Click XP/h
  to rank every run by its experience per hour, best first, across all dungeons; click again to
  turn the order. A run's share of a level stands in the line under it.
- ForeverDungeonXP now needs RazzhUI installed, and is switched on or off with its checkbox on the
  RazzhUI page (Options → AddOns → RazzhUI).
