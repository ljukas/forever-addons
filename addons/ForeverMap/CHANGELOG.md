# ForeverMap

## Unreleased

- Herbs and ore that Find Herbs and Find Minerals show on the minimap get their tracking
  icon in the minimap's gold ring, instead of a plain yellow dot. The icons stay on their
  nodes as you move and turn.
- A node's icon disappears once you've looted it.
- Nodes in caves get a dimmed icon.
- Other addons' minimap buttons are gathered behind one button on the minimap's edge. Click it
  to fold the buttons out, click again to fold them away.
- Drag the button around the minimap's edge to put it where you like, and choose which way
  the buttons open (down, up, left or right) in the options.
- Right-click the button, or use "Choose buttons" in the options, to pick the buttons that
  should stay on the minimap instead.
- Turn the button off under Options → AddOns → ForeverMap and every button goes back where
  it was.
- Turn the icons on or off and change their size under Options → AddOns → ForeverMap.
