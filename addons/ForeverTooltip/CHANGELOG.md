# ForeverTooltip

## Unreleased

- When you hover a player, their name shows in their class colour.
- The level line below it turns red, yellow or green to show whether the player is an enemy, attackable or friendly, the colours the name used to have.
- New option in Options → AddOns → ForeverTooltip: "Tooltip follows the cursor" shows tooltips for players, creatures and objects in the world next to your mouse instead of in the corner. Off by default.
