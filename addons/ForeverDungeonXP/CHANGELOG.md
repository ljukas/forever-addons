# ForeverDungeonXP

## Unreleased

- A journal of your dungeon runs: the experience each run's kills gave you, how big a share of a
  level that was, the kills per creature, the time from first to last kill and the experience
  per hour. Open it from the minimap button, the addon menu on the minimap or with `/fdxp`.
- Elites, rares and bosses are marked in a run's creature list with the dragon and skull icons,
  for runs recorded from this version on. Click a column heading to sort the list by it; click
  again to turn the order.
- A run shows who you ran it with: each party member's name and level in their class colour,
  with class and group role in the line's tooltip. Recorded for runs from this version on.
