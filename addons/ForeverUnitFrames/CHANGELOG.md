# ForeverUnitFrames

## Unreleased

- The player frame's power bar (mana, rage, energy) becomes a bar of its own, framed like the cast bar and above the action bars by default. Move it in Edit Mode, where it snaps like the other boxes, and set its width and height in its Edit Mode dialog; each Edit Mode layout keeps its own. Its Smooth Fill checkbox makes the bar slide to each new value instead of jumping to it.
- The player frame's health bar grows to fill the space the power bar leaves.
