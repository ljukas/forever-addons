# ForeverMinimap

## Unreleased

- Now grouped under "Forever Enhanced Addons" in the AddOns list.
- Herbs and ore that Find Herbs and Find Minerals show on the minimap get their tracking
  icon in the minimap's gold ring, instead of a plain yellow dot. The icons stay on their
  nodes as you move and turn.
- A node's icon disappears once you've looted it.
- Poor Copper Veins get the ore icon too.
- Each node's icon shows what it gives: Copper Ore on a Copper Vein, Peacebloom on Peacebloom,
  and so on, still inside the gold tracking ring.
- Nodes in caves get a dimmed icon.
- The icons grow and shrink with the minimap's Icon Scale in Edit Mode, like the game's own
  minimap icons. Turn them on or off under Options → AddOns → ForeverMinimap.
- Looking for nodes on the minimap takes about half the CPU it did, and much less while you
  stand still, without the short spikes every second or two.
- Other addons' minimap buttons are gathered behind one button on the minimap's edge. Click it
  to fold the buttons out, click again to fold them away.
- Drag the button around the minimap's edge to put it where you like, and choose which way
  the buttons open (down, up, left or right) in the options.
- Right-click the button, or use "Choose buttons" in the options, to pick the buttons that
  should stay on the minimap instead.
- Turn the button off under Options → AddOns → ForeverMinimap and every button goes back where
  it was.
- The button has the same gold ring as other addons' minimap buttons, and buttons that sit on
  the minimap's edge without belonging to it, such as Cinematic's, are gathered too.
- The gathered buttons now show above the quest tracker and other frames near the minimap.
- Choose how big the gathered buttons are with the "Button size" slider in the options. By
  default they now open at the size of a standard minimap button, a little bigger than before.
