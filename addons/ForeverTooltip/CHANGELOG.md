# ForeverTooltip

## Unreleased

- When you hover a player, their name shows in their class colour.
- Their level line is coloured by how their level compares to yours, like quest difficulty: gray (far below you), green, yellow (around your level), orange, red (well above you, or ?? level).
- Creatures and NPCs get the same level colours, and a ?? level shows a skull instead, like on the target frame.
- Their Alliance/Horde line shows whether they're friend or foe: red for enemies, yellow for players you can attack who aren't hostile, green for allies.
- New option in Options → AddOns → ForeverTooltip: "Tooltip follows the cursor" shows tooltips for players, creatures and objects in the world next to your mouse instead of at the usual tooltip spot. Off by default. When it's on, "Tooltip grows towards" picks which side of the cursor the tooltip appears on: down and right, down and left, up and right (the default) or up and left.
